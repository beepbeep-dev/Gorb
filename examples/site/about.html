How it works

  1. Your browser asks for gorb://welcome.gorb/about.html.
  2. The request is turned into 1200/2200 Hz tones (like old-school packet radio) and transmitted.
  3. Any station in range repeats it, so it can reach hosts far away.
  4. The host compresses the page, chops it into small chunks and sends them back.
  5. Your browser collects the chunks, asks again for any it missed, and shows the page.
